01 / Research

Connecting hardware
to intelligence.

Real hardware brings noise, variation, and other non-idealities. My research asks how these effects propagate from memory devices and circuits to neural-network accuracy—and how we can design around them.

I combine PyTorch-based workload evaluation with SPICE and Cadence circuit validation, studying both CMOS and emerging-memory technologies.
